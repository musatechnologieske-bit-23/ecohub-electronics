First time using Ecohub?

A few tips before your first transaction

  • Start at the dashboard. Use the sidebar to move between customers, inventory, invoices, and quotations. Select your name's role label to understand your access: cashiers handle daily sales; admins also manage products, stock, staff, and reports.
  • Check before creating. Search for the customer or product first to avoid duplicates. If a product or customer is missing, ask an admin to add it or use the available customer option when preparing a quotation.
  • Use a draft while details are uncertain. Drafts let you review a document before issuing it. Issuing an invoice deducts catalog stock; quotations and draft invoices do not.
  • Record payments on the invoice. Enter the amount actually received. Partial payments keep a balance open; a receipt is created when the invoice is fully paid.
  • Keep references useful. Add supplier or payment references where available, and check Stock History if a quantity does not look right.
  • If you cannot see an action, it may be restricted to admins. Ask your administrator to confirm your role rather than sharing another person's login.

Getting started

The usual order for setting up and using the system

  1. 01 / Prepare

    Admins set up categories, products, prices, and opening stock. Add customers as needed.

  2. 02 / Transact

    Create a quotation for approval or issue an invoice for a completed sale. Record payments against invoices.

  3. 03 / Review

    Check stock history and reports to reconcile inventory and follow sales activity.

Inventory

Keep the catalog and stock quantities accurate

Open inventory

Create a category and product Admin

  1. Open Categories and add a category name; description is optional.
  2. Return to Inventory and choose Add Product.
  3. Enter the product name, category, cost price, selling price, and reorder level. Leave SKU blank to generate one.
  4. Enter starting stock if this is a new item, then save. Starting quantity is recorded in stock history.

Receive more stock Admin

  1. Open Stock-In from Inventory.
  2. Select the product and enter the quantity received. Add a supplier or purchase-order reference when available.
  3. Submit the stock-in. Review the new entry under Stock History.

Sales of catalog products deduct stock when an invoice is issued. Draft invoices and quotations do not deduct stock.

Import products from Excel Admin

  1. Choose Import Excel on the Inventory page.
  2. Select an `.xlsx` or `.xls` file and submit it.
  3. Review the import result, then check product records and quantities in Inventory.

Find low or out-of-stock items

  1. Open Inventory and use the stock-level filter, category filter, or product search.
  2. Choose Low Stock or Out of Stock to narrow the list.
  3. Admins can open a product and use Stock-In to record replenishment.

Customers

Maintain customer contact and account details

Open customers
  1. 1. Open Customers and choose Add Customer.
  2. 2. Enter the person or company name; account number, phone, email, address, and notes can also be recorded.
  3. 3. Save, then search the customer list by name or account details to find the record later.
  4. 4. Open the customer record to review its details and related sales, or edit the record to keep contact information current.

Sales, invoices, and receipts

Create documents, record payments, and print customer copies

Open sales

Prepare a quotation

  1. Open Quotations and select Create Quotation.
  2. Select an existing customer, add a new customer, or leave the customer blank for a walk-in.
  3. Add catalog products or ad-hoc items, then set quantities, discount, dates, and customer terms.
  4. Save as a draft or create the quotation. Open it to print or, when approved, convert it to an invoice.

Quotation tax is calculated at 16%. Creating or converting a quotation does not deduct stock; conversion to an invoice does.

Issue an invoice

  1. Open Sales & Invoices and start a new invoice.
  2. Select a customer, or leave the customer empty for a walk-in sale. Set issue and optional due dates.
  3. Add catalog products or custom line items. Confirm quantities, prices, discount, tax, and notes.
  4. Save as a draft to continue later, or issue the invoice to finalize the sale.
  5. Open the invoice to print it, record payment, and review its current balance.

Record a payment and issue a receipt

  1. Open the invoice and choose the payment action.
  2. Enter a positive amount no greater than the balance due, then select the payment method and add a reference if needed.
  3. Save the payment. Partial payments update the balance and status.
  4. When the balance reaches zero, the system generates an official receipt. Open the receipt from the invoice to print it.

Locate or print a sales document

  1. Use the Invoices or Quotations list to search and filter documents.
  2. Open a document to view its items, status, customer, payments, and balance.
  3. Use its print action for a customer copy; completed payments also provide a receipt print action.

Administration

Admin-only controls for staff and business reporting

Create or update a staff account

  1. Open Staff Management and choose Add User.
  2. Enter the login details and select the role: Admin for management access or Cashier for daily sales access.
  3. Save the account. Use Edit to update details or the account status control to disable or re-enable access.
  4. Give the new staff member their login details securely. Do not share administrator credentials.

Run and export reports

  1. Open Reports.
  2. Set the available reporting period and filters, then review the sales and inventory summaries.
  3. Use the print view for a paper/PDF copy, or export sales data to CSV or Excel for further analysis.

Good operating habits

  • Record each delivery through Stock-In so stock history remains traceable.
  • Use drafts while details are being confirmed; issued invoices deduct catalog stock.
  • Record payments against the invoice so balances and receipts stay in sync.
  • Check the stock movement history when quantities need to be reconciled.
